# Little Lemon App
Little Lemon is a modern restaurant web app that makes dining effortless and enjoyable. Designed with React, the app allows users to book tables with ease, browse the menu, and place online orders—all from a clean, responsive interface.

Built for convenience, Little Lemon streamlines the restaurant experience with user-friendly booking forms, dynamic menu rendering, and smooth navigation. Whether you're planning a night out or ordering your favorite dishes from home, Little Lemon delivers speed, simplicity, and style.
